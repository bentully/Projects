Our Roots

Verix Barbershop was founded in 2025 by Dillon Palmer, a barber who grew an immense passion for the craft. The vision was to create a barbershop that not only provided top-notch grooming services but also fostered a sense of community among its clients. Open from 9am to 6pm, we are committed to providing a calm and collected environment where you can relax and enjoy a premium grooming experience.
